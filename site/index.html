About

Spotlit

Version 1.0 for macOS 13 Ventura or later

Spotlit collects no data. There is no account and no tracking, and it works offline.

It asks for Input Monitoring only when you turn on keystrokes, and for Screen Recording only when you turn on the magnifier.
