Spotlit for Mac

Spotlit lives in your menu bar and puts a highlight around your mouse pointer, so people can follow it during screen shares, recordings and presentations. PRO adds extras such as keystroke display and a magnifier.

Questions

How do I open Spotlit?

Click the Spotlit icon in the menu bar. If you open the app again from Applications, it shows the Settings window.

Is there a shortcut to turn it on and off?

Yes, press ⌃⌥S (Control + Option + S).

My keystrokes don't show up

Allow Spotlit under Input Monitoring in System Settings › Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen Spotlit.

The magnifier shows nothing

Allow Spotlit under Screen Recording in System Settings › Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen Spotlit.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings › Spotlit PRO and choose Restore Purchase.

How does the free trial work?

You get 3 days of every PRO feature. After that the PRO features lock until you buy the one-time unlock. Your settings are kept.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Spotlit and move it to the Bin. Its settings are in ~/Library/Containers/in.kjrlabs.spotlit.
